languages in between. I'm also a good instructor and have instructed
hundreds of courses for many tech companies in Israel and around
the world. I am an architect and not only a coder and have been
involved in designing the architecture for several products: a home
automation system for Intel, a very scalable website for Yahoo, a real
time application for Rafael, AWS scalable processing app for my own
private clients and more. I instructed about 150 different courses,
syllabus wise, each several times, during the last decade usually for
professionals in the industry. From methodological courses like agile
programming to very technical courses, like multi-core programming

introducing free source to companies. I am a self learner on most of
the topics that I instruct and use. I have a strong math background (I
have not achieved M.Sc but only due to laziness). I have managed
teams on various projects but I can also work alone. When I manage
a team I try to avoid bureaucratic practices and be part of the team.
I have contributed to many free and open source projects like the
Linux kernel and I am currently the maintainer of GNU grep(1). I
code as a past time on pet projects which you can find on github. My
favourite areas are the Linux kernel, UNIX architecture and building
embedded Linux systems. I also do my own system administration
and have taught many system administration courses. I am certified
for mysql, solaris, Linux, AWS and some more stuff. I am also a
professional MySQL DBA, offered professional support for it and

Lately I have been doing data science, machine learning, and NLP
on average sized data (few terabytes).
